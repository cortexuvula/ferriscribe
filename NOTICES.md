### Bundled medical wordlist

- Bundled as: `src/lib/components/rich_editor/spellcheck/medical_terms.txt`
- Generated by: `scripts/build_medical_wordlist.mjs`
- Upstream source: <https://github.com/CodeSante/medical-wordlist>
  (`en/wordlist.en.txt`)
- Upstream license: WTFPL (Do What The F*ck You Want To Public License,
  v2 — a permissive license effectively equivalent to public-domain
  dedication).
- Upstream provenance: derived from Wikidata SPARQL queries; Wikidata
  is published under CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication).
- Local processing: the upstream multi-word file is tokenized into
  individual ASCII letter words (length ≥ 3), lowercased, deduped, and
  sorted by the build script. No additional creative content is added.

Re-run `node scripts/build_medical_wordlist.mjs` to refresh from upstream.
